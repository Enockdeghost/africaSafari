Mafia Island

Indian Ocean

Tanzania's Marine Paradise – The Undiscovered Island Gem

Welcome to Mafia Island, Tanzania's best-kept secret in the Indian Ocean. At True Africa Escape, we invite you to discover this pristine archipelago where turquoise waters meet untouched coral reefs in one of Africa's most spectacular marine parks. Unlike its busier neighbors, Mafia offers an authentic island experience with world-class diving, fascinating history, and barefoot luxury.

Part of the Mafia Archipelago in Tanzania's Pwani Region, this island combines rich Swahili culture with exceptional marine biodiversity. The Mafia Island Marine Park protects some of the healthiest coral reefs in East Africa, making it a haven for divers, snorkelers, and those seeking tranquil beaches away from mass tourism.

435 km² Island Size
400+ Fish Species
Oct-May Best Time to Visit

Island Highlights

  • Home to Tanzania's first marine park with pristine coral reefs and abundant marine life
  • One of the world's best destinations for swimming with whale sharks (Oct-Mar)
  • Rich Swahili history with ruins dating back to the 11th century
  • Authentic island culture without mass tourism development
  • Diverse accommodations from eco-lodges to boutique beach resorts

Top Activities in Mafia Island

Scuba Diving

Explore world-class dive sites in Mafia Marine Park with healthy coral gardens, reef sharks, turtles, and exceptional visibility (Oct-May).

Whale Shark Encounters

Swim with gentle whale sharks (Oct-Mar) in one of the few places where these majestic creatures aggregate in predictable numbers.

Snorkeling

Discover vibrant coral reefs just meters from shore, with excellent sites around Chole Bay and the marine park.

Dhow Safaris

Traditional sailing trips to sandbanks and remote islands aboard wooden dhows with seafood lunches and snorkeling stops.

Deep Sea Fishing

Chase marlin, sailfish, and tuna in the deep waters beyond the reef with experienced local guides.

Birdwatching

Spot over 120 bird species including crab plovers and mangrove kingfishers in Mafia's diverse coastal habitats.

Cultural Tours

Visit historic sites like Kua Ruins on Juani Island and traditional fishing villages to experience authentic Swahili culture.

Best Time to Visit Mafia Island

Mafia offers different experiences throughout the year:

  • October – March: Whale shark season with calm seas and best diving conditions
  • April – May: Shoulder season with fewer visitors and good marine life
  • June – September: Cooler and windier but excellent for kitesurfing and fishing
  • July – August: Humpback whale migration passes nearby (possible sightings)

True Africa Escape can advise on the best time for your specific interests in Mafia Island.

Daily flights from Dar es Salaam to Mafia Island Airport (30-45 minutes) or charter flights from Zanzibar.

By Sea:

Weekly passenger ferries from Dar es Salaam (6-8 hours) or private boat charters available.

Tarangire National Park

Northern Circuit

The Land of Giants – Tanzania's Hidden Safari Gem

Welcome to Tarangire National Park, Tanzania's elephant paradise and one of the most biodiverse wildlife areas in the country. At True Africa Escape, we invite you to discover this underrated gem of the Northern Circuit, famous for its massive baobab trees, large elephant herds, and exceptional year-round game viewing away from the crowds.

Located just a short drive from Arusha, Tarangire offers a more intimate safari experience compared to its famous neighbors. The park's namesake river provides a lifeline during the dry season, creating spectacular wildlife concentrations that make for unforgettable game drives.

2,850 km² Park Size
550+ Bird Species
June-October Peak Season

Park Highlights

  • Home to Tanzania's largest elephant population with herds of up to 300 individuals
  • Iconic baobab-studded landscapes creating dramatic photographic backdrops
  • Exceptional dry season (June-Oct) when wildlife concentrates along the Tarangire River
  • Less crowded than other Northern Circuit parks offering more exclusive game viewing
  • Diverse habitats from swamps to savannah supporting varied wildlife

Tarangire offers rewarding wildlife viewing year-round, but seasons vary:

Selous Game Reserve

Southern Circuit

Africa's Largest Game Reserve – A Wilderness Untamed

Welcome to Selous Game Reserve, Tanzania's vast wilderness sanctuary and UNESCO World Heritage Site. At True Africa Escape, we invite you to experience this extraordinary ecosystem where the mighty Rufiji River winds through untouched landscapes teeming with wildlife. Covering 5% of Tanzania's total area, Selous offers a safari experience unlike any other in Africa.

Located in southern Tanzania, Selous remains one of the continent's last great wilderness areas. With its diverse habitats ranging from grassy plains to dense woodlands and meandering waterways, Selous provides a sanctuary for significant populations of elephants, wild dogs, and other iconic African species.

50,000 km² Reserve Size
440+ Bird Species
Jun-Oct Best Time to Visit

Reserve Highlights

  • Africa's largest protected wildlife reserve and less visited than northern parks
  • Home to one-third of all African wild dogs, the continent's most endangered predator
  • Boat safaris along the Rufiji River system offering unique water-level wildlife viewing
  • Walking safaris in designated areas for an immersive wilderness experience
  • Remote luxury camps providing exclusive access to pristine wilderness areas

Ngorongoro Crater

Northern Circuit

Africa's Greatest Natural Wonder

Welcome to Ngorongoro Crater, Tanzania's geological marvel where nature's grandeur and Africa's wildlife heritage converge in spectacular fashion. At True Africa Escape, we believe Ngorongoro is not just a destination—it's an immersive experience that captures the soul of Tanzania's wilderness and culture.

Formed over two million years ago by the collapse of an ancient volcano in northern Tanzania, Ngorongoro is the world's largest intact volcanic caldera, stretching over 260 square kilometers. Its towering crater walls rise almost 600 meters, creating a natural amphitheater teeming with wildlife and breathtaking panoramas.

260 km² Crater Size
25,000+ Large Mammals
Year-round Best Time to Visit

Crater Highlights

  • The Big Five in Tanzania: Lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo, and endangered black rhinos
  • Highest density of predators in Africa within Ngorongoro Conservation Area
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site status protecting this unique ecosystem
  • Cultural encounters with Maasai communities living in Ngorongoro
  • Diverse landscapes from grasslands to forests within the crater floor
