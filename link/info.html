General Overview

  • Location: East Africa, bordered by Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, and the Indian Ocean
  • Capital: Dodoma (largest city Dar es Salaam)
  • Official Languages: Swahili and English
  • Currency: Tanzanian Shilling (TZS)
  • Time Zone: East Africa Time (EAT), UTC +3

Top Tourist Attractions

  • Serengeti National Park: Famous for the Great Migration and abundant wildlife
  • Ngorongoro Crater: Unique volcanic crater with dense wildlife populations
  • Mount Kilimanjaro: Africa's highest peak, popular trekking destination
  • Zanzibar Archipelago: White sandy beaches, rich culture, and historic Stone Town
  • Tarangire National Park: Known for large elephant herds and baobab trees
  • Lake Manyara National Park: Famous for tree-climbing lions and flamingos
  • Selous Game Reserve: One of the largest faunal reserves in the world
  • Ruaha National Park: Untouched wilderness and large prides of lions

Best Time to Visit

  • Dry Season (June to October): Best for safaris and trekking (cooler, less rain)
  • Short Rainy Season (Nov-Dec): Wildlife viewing remains good
  • Long Rainy Season (March-May): Less ideal for travel due to heavy rains

Visa Requirements

  • Most visitors require a visa (can be obtained on arrival or online e-visa)
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months from date of entry
  • Visa fees vary by nationality

Health & Safety

  • Vaccinations: Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from endemic countries
  • Recommended: Hepatitis A, Typhoid, Tetanus, Malaria prophylaxis
  • Use insect repellent and mosquito nets to prevent malaria
  • Drink bottled or purified water only
  • Basic medical facilities available in cities; carry travel insurance

Transportation

  • Air: Kilimanjaro International Airport (KIA), Julius Nyerere International Airport (Dar es Salaam)
  • Road: Well-connected by buses and private transfers; roads can be rough in rural areas
  • Safari Vehicles: 4x4 vehicles common for game drives
  • Boats: Used for Zanzibar and lake tours

Accommodation

  • Options range from budget hostels and campsites to luxury lodges and resorts
  • Popular safari lodges offer all-inclusive packages
  • Zanzibar has a wide range of beachfront resorts and boutique hotels

Currency & Payments

  • Tanzanian Shilling (TZS) is the local currency
  • US Dollars widely accepted, especially in tourism areas
  • Credit cards accepted in major hotels and lodges but carry cash in rural areas
  • ATMs available in major towns

Culture & Etiquette

  • Tanzania is culturally diverse with over 120 ethnic groups
  • Dress modestly, especially in rural areas and Zanzibar (predominantly Muslim)
  • Greetings are important—shake hands or use local greetings
  • Ask permission before photographing people, especially in villages

Useful Tips

  • Learn basic Swahili phrases like "Jambo" (Hello), "Asante" (Thank you)
  • Carry photocopies of passport and travel documents
  • Respect wildlife and follow park rules
  • Bargaining is common in markets and small shops
  • Tipping guides and drivers is customary

Safari Planning Guide

Tanzania Best Parks to Visit:

  • Serengeti National Park: Great Migration, Big Five
  • Kilimanjaro Mountain
  • Ngorongoro Crater: High wildlife density in a unique ecosystem
  • Tarangire National Park: Elephants, baobabs, fewer crowds
  • Lake Manyara: Tree-climbing lions, flamingos
  • Selous & Ruaha (Southern Circuit): Remote, untamed wilderness

Recommended Safari Duration:

  • 4-5 days: Short safari
  • 7-10 days: Full Northern or Southern circuit
  • 12+ days: Luxury or photographic safaris

Safari Types:

  • Budget Camping Safari
  • Mid-range Lodge Safari
  • Luxury Fly-in Safari
  • Private Family or Honeymoon Safari
  • VIP
  • Special Groups Convoys

What to Pack:

  • Neutral-colored clothing
  • Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • Binoculars, camera with zoom
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Insect repellent, travel insurance

Cultural Tour Guide

Top Cultural Experiences:

  • Maasai Village Visits (Arusha/Ngorongoro): Learn traditions, dance, crafts
  • Hadzabe & Datoga Tribes (Lake Eyasi): Ancient hunting & blacksmithing
  • Chagga Village Tour (Moshi): Coffee-making, caves, waterfalls
  • Zanzibar Stone Town Tour: Swahili-Arabic culture, food, historic sites
  • Bagamoyo & Kilwa Ruins: UNESCO heritage and old slave trade ports

Tips for Cultural Tours:

  • Always ask before taking photos
  • Dress modestly in villages and religious areas
  • Support community-run initiatives and local guides

Zanzibar Beach Holiday Guide

Best Beaches:

  • Nungwi & Kendwa (North): Great for swimming, luxury resorts
  • Paje & Jambiani (East): Kitesurfing, beach bars, relaxed vibe
  • Kizimkazi (South): Dolphin tours, cultural experiences
  • Stone Town (West): Cultural base, museums, and spice tours

Activities:

  • Snorkeling & Diving (Mnemba Atoll)
  • Spice Farm Tours
  • Jozani Forest - Red Colobus Monkeys
  • Prison Island - Tortoises & snorkeling
  • Sunset Dhow Cruises
  • Sandbank Picnics

Ideal Length of Stay:

  • 3-5 days: Stone Town & beach combo
  • 7+ days: Full relaxation, water sports, cultural tours
