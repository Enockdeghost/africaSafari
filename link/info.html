General Overview

  • Location: East Africa, bordered by Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, Zambia, Malawi, Mozambique, and the Indian Ocean
  • Capital: Dodoma (largest city Dar es Salaam)
  • Official Languages: Swahili and English
  • Currency: Tanzanian Shilling (TZS)
  • Time Zone: East Africa Time (EAT), UTC +3

Top Tourist Attractions

  • Serengeti National Park: Famous for the Great Migration and abundant wildlife
  • Ngorongoro Crater: Unique volcanic crater with dense wildlife populations
  • Mount Kilimanjaro: Africa's highest peak, popular trekking destination
  • Zanzibar Archipelago: White sandy beaches, rich culture, and historic Stone Town
  • Tarangire National Park: Known for large elephant herds and baobab trees
  • Lake Manyara National Park: Famous for tree-climbing lions and flamingos
  • Selous Game Reserve: One of the largest faunal reserves in the world
  • Ruaha National Park: Untouched wilderness and large prides of lions

Best Time to Visit

  • Dry Season (June to October): Best for safaris and trekking (cooler, less rain)
  • Short Rainy Season (Nov-Dec): Wildlife viewing remains good
  • Long Rainy Season (March-May): Less ideal for travel due to heavy rains

Visa Requirements

  • Most visitors require a visa (can be obtained on arrival or online e-visa)
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months from date of entry
  • Visa fees vary by nationality

Health & Safety

  • Vaccinations: Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from endemic countries
  • Recommended: Hepatitis A, Typhoid, Tetanus, Malaria prophylaxis
  • Use insect repellent and mosquito nets to prevent malaria
  • Drink bottled or purified water only
  • Basic medical facilities available in cities; carry travel insurance

Safari Planning Guide

Tanzania Best Parks to Visit:

  • Serengeti National Park: Great Migration, Big Five
  • Kilimanjaro Mountain
  • Ngorongoro Crater: High wildlife density in a unique ecosystem
  • Tarangire National Park: Elephants, baobabs, fewer crowds
  • Lake Manyara: Tree-climbing lions, flamingos
  • Selous & Ruaha (Southern Circuit): Remote, untamed wilderness

Recommended Safari Duration:

  • 4-5 days: Short safari
  • 7-10 days: Full Northern or Southern circuit
  • 12+ days: Luxury or photographic safaris

Safari Types:

  • Budget Camping Safari
  • Mid-range Lodge Safari
  • Luxury Fly-in Safari
  • Private Family or Honeymoon Safari
  • VIP
  • Special Groups Convoys

What to Pack:

  • Neutral-colored clothing
  • Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • Binoculars, camera with zoom
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Insect repellent, travel insurance
